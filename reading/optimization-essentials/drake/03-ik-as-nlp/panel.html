Obstacle sphere (c, ro)

A change re-solves the current target. The slide's cue numbers and the presets other than "inside the sphere" assume the default sphere. A sphere that overlaps the base or link 1 can make every target infeasible.

Active rows and multipliers

rowgapλ

—

A row is active when its gap is below 10⁻⁶ m (a sphere) or 10⁻⁵ rad (a joint), not when its multiplier looks large. Growing ro by δ raises f* by about (Σλ) δ; moving the target by δ along x changes f* by about −κx δ.
