Sampling demo: a tree grows through the configuration space of the two-link arm

This page grows a rapidly exploring random tree (RRT) from the start posture of the arm (links 0.4 m and 0.3 m) toward the goal posture, the positive-elbow solution of the tip target (0.5, 0.2) m. In the default model no part of either link may come within 5 mm of the disc, and the straight edge between the two postures is invalid. Each Step draws one random configuration, finds the nearest tree node, proposes an extension of at most 10°, checks that edge with configurations at most 1° apart in each joint, and keeps or rejects it. Each posture drawn on the left is one point on the right. Hover over the right view to see the posture at the mouse.
