Frames and monogram notation

CS 549 · session 2.2 · drag the tray frame T or the mug M; turn the tray with the slider. Units: metres, degrees.

Controls

30°

Readouts

The three readouts of the arrow tray→mug describe one arrow. Only the subscript, the frame whose axes the numbers are written in, changes. With the mug attached, turning the slider leaves TpMT unchanged and changes TpMW and WpM.

Default state = the worked example of the session 2.2 notes §5: T at (0.40, 0.20), θT = 30°, mug at (0.15, 0.05) in T. Companion notes: notes.md.
