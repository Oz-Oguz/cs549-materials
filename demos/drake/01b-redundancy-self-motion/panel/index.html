How to use the robot

Choose Open Controls in Meshcat. Drag one joint slider, in degrees, and watch the other six compensate.

The held point is 0.1034 m beyond the flange along its z axis, between the fingertips. The flange can move, and gripper rotation is free. Fingertip error is measured at the displayed pose.

  • Random pose, same tip finds another posture.
  • Play / pause wander starts or pauses continuous motion.
  • Reset (elbow up) restores the starting posture.
  • Show / hide wrist trail shows where the flange has moved.
  • Show / hide Jacobian columns toggles the seven position columns.
  • Stop ends the demo.
