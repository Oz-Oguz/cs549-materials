What one Step shows

At iterate i the solid arm is at q(i) and the faded ghost at q(i−1). The linear model built at q(i−1) promised the flange at the teal point; forward kinematics put it at the maroon point, the current flange. Their distance is the prediction gap: the local model's error for this update. The gold sphere is the target; small gold beads mark every past flange position.

objectivef(q) = ½ ‖φ(q) − y*‖²
stepΔq(i−1) = α (J⊤J + ρ I)−1 J⊤ (y* − φ(q(i−1)))
step lengthα = 1 without a line search; Armijo shortens α until the cost drops enough
predictionp(i−1) + J Δq(i−1), with J and p(i−1) = φ(q(i−1)) taken at the previous iterate
resultφ(q(i)), with q(i) = q(i−1) + Δq(i−1)

ρ damps the proposed joint increment inside the step; it is not part of the objective. J is the 3×7 translational Jacobian of the flange from Drake.

Suggested sequence

  1. Run 2, Step several times: the gap shrinks faster than the error; the model earns trust as the steps shrink.
  2. Run 3b, slider to a late iterate: the gap is below one micrometer while the task error stays near 0.25 m. An accurate local prediction can go with a large remaining error.
  3. Only when discussing Armijo: tick "show rejected trials" at an early iterate of run 3b. Grey beads are evaluated candidates the arm never moved through.
